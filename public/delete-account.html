Delete your Schoolink account

You can delete your account and data at any time. Schoolink does not keep a hidden copy.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Schoolink and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete account.
  3. Type DELETE (and your password if you use email) and tap Delete my account.

Can't open the app?

Email support@schoolink.app from the email address on your account with the subject Delete my Schoolink account. We will confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, photos and bio
  • Your posts, comments, statuses and saved items
  • Your chats and messages you sent
  • Your follows, communities and notification settings
  • Your login history and device notification token

What may be kept

  • Reports, bans and appeals, where they are needed to protect other people
  • Marks and records that belong to your school (ask your school about these)
