# 🎓 Schoolink

> *Where every school finds its voice, and every voice finds its school.*

---

## What is this?

Schoolink is a social platform built for schools — not another generic app wearing a school's colors, but something designed from the ground up around how a school actually works: Headteachers who need oversight, teachers who need to reach their students, students who need to find their people, and parents who just want to know what's going on.

Think of it as the intersection of a school notice board, a staff room conversation, a student council meeting, and a family group chat — except it scales from one classroom to thousands of schools, each with its own walled garden of trust.

Built entirely from a phone, one commit at a time.

## The team behind the platform

Schoolink has its own **Control Center**, a separate dark dashboard that only the platform team can open. Team members are hidden from normal users and sign in to the Control Center instead of the regular app.

| Team role | What they handle |
|---|---|
| 👑 **Owner** | Everything: team invites, site activity, and announcements |
| ✅ **Verifier** | The verification queue for IDs and school documents |
| 🛡️ **Moderator** | User reports, complaints, banning accounts |
| 💬 **Support** | Help and feedback messages |

- Team members join by **invite** and can change their email, password and profile photo from their own Control Center profile.
- Roles are tied to the account itself, so changing an email never loses access.
- The owner can post **48-hour announcements** (text, multiple photos, videos with sound, and files). They appear first in everyone's Home feed from "Schoolink" with the verified badge, and the name can't be clicked.

## What actually works

- 🔐 **Real accounts** — email/password and Google sign-in, not a demo login
- 🏫 **One app, many schools** — each with its own members, posts, and identity
- ✉️ **Invitations, not open doors** — staff join by code, students by a school-wide code with approval
- 📰 **A feed that feels alive** — posts, photos, categories, likes, comments, follows, saves
- 💬 **Real-time chat** — read receipts, online status, blocking
- 🔵 **Verified badges** — a Meta-style blue seal for verified people and schools
- ⏳ **24-hour statuses** — share a moment that disappears on its own
- 🎓 **Alumni directory** — search former students by name or occupation and filter by class year
- 🚩 **Reports and account deletion** — flag what doesn't belong, and delete your account whenever you like
- 👀 **Guest mode** — browse, search and read updates without an account; following, posting, messaging and downloads ask you to sign in, and help and feedback still work
- 🏘️ **Communities** — the group-chat feeling, built for subject teachers and sports coaches
- 📅 **A calendar that RSVPs back**
- 📖 **A library that crosses borders** — Shona novels next to Cambridge past papers
- 🎨 **A theme that's actually yours** — pick a color, flip on dark mode, make it feel like home
- 📊 **Polls** — ask a question and let the school vote
- 🏅 **Achievements** — celebrate students, teams and staff
- 🧠 **Counselling and reception tools** — dedicated dashboards for the people who look after students and visitors
- 🔔 **Notifications and search** — find people, schools and posts, and never miss a reply
- 🔒 **Account safety** — login history, two-factor settings, blocking and favourite chats
- 🛠️ **Help and feedback** — message the Schoolink team straight from the app
- 🌙 **A modern look** — dark side drawer and bottom navigation with a glowing active tab and unread badges
- ℹ️ **About Schoolink** — what the app is, help, terms, privacy and a check for updates
- 📱 **Installable** — add it to your home screen, it forgets it's a website

## Built differently

This whole project was built and debugged entirely on a mobile phone — no laptop, no local dev server, no shortcuts. Every file was typed or pasted directly into a mobile editor, every bug hunted through screenshots of build logs, every deploy watched live from a phone screen at odd hours.

If that sounds impossible, that's rather the point.
