# 🎓 Schoolink

> *Where every school finds its voice, and every voice finds its school.*

---

## What is this?

Schoolink is a social platform built for schools — not another generic app wearing a school's colors, but something designed from the ground up around how a school actually works: Headteachers who need oversight, teachers who need to reach their students, students who need to find their people, and parents who just want to know what's going on.

Think of it as the intersection of a school notice board, a staff room conversation, a student council meeting, and a family group chat — except it scales from one classroom to thousands of schools, each with its own walled garden of trust.

Built entirely from a phone, one commit at a time.

---

## The world inside Schoolink

**Thirteen kinds of people**, each seeing a different shape of the same app:

| Role | What they see |
|---|---|
| 🎓 **Headteacher** | Everything. The keys to the kingdom. |
| 🧭 **Deputy Head** | Almost everything, minus the power to hand off the crown. |
| 💰 **Bursar** | Fees, income, the numbers that keep the lights on. |
| 🏆 **School Coach** | Fixtures, results, the scoreboard of school pride. |
| 💻 **ICT Administrator** | Every device, every login, the invisible plumbing. |
| 📚 **Librarian** | A living catalog — novels, textbooks, decades of past papers. |
| 🧑‍🏫 **Teacher** | Assignments, timetables, a classroom that never quite closes. |
| 🧑‍🎓 **Student** | A feed, a library, a place to belong — nothing more, nothing less. |
| 👨‍👩‍👧 **Parent** | A window into the school, without needing to walk through the gate. |
| 💼 **Investor** | A handshake, formalized. |
| 👋 **Guest** | A glimpse, an invitation waiting to happen. |

Every role is enforced where it counts — not just hidden buttons in the interface, but real boundaries underneath.

## What actually works

- 🔐 **Real accounts** — email/password and Google sign-in, not a demo login
- 🏫 **One app, many schools** — each with its own members, posts, and identity
- ✉️ **Invitations, not open doors** — staff join by code, students by a school-wide code with approval
- 📰 **A feed that feels alive** — posts, photos, categories, likes, comments, follows, saves
- 💬 **Real-time chat** — read receipts, online status, blocking
- 🏘️ **Communities** — the group-chat feeling, built for subject teachers and sports coaches
- 📅 **A calendar that RSVPs back**
- 📖 **A library that crosses borders** — Shona novels next to Cambridge past papers
- 🎨 **A theme that's actually yours** — pick a color, flip on dark mode, make it feel like home
- 📱 **Installable** — add it to your home screen, it forgets it's a website

## Built differently

This whole project was built and debugged entirely on a mobile phone — no laptop, no local dev server, no shortcuts. Every file was typed or pasted directly into a mobile editor, every bug hunted through screenshots of build logs, every deploy watched live from a phone screen at odd hours.

If that sounds impossible, that's rather the point.
